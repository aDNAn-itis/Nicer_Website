Neutron star interior composition

The Neutron star Interior Composition Explorer is a NASA Astrophysics Mission of Opportunity in the Explorer program. It launched aboard SpaceX CRS-11 and was installed as an external payload on the space station.

Neutron stars are collapsed stellar cores that can contain more mass than the Sun in a sphere only about 20 kilometers across. By studying their X-ray pulses, NICER helps constrain the relationship between mass, radius, and the behavior of matter at nuclear densities.

PRIMARY MISSION OBJECTIVE

Measure neutron star radii to help constrain the equation of state of dense matter.
