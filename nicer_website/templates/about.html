EXTREME MATTER / EXTRAORDINARY PHYSICS

The NICER Mission

Neutron stars are among the densest objects in the universe. NICER studies their structure and dynamics through precise X-ray timing measurements from its home on the International Space Station.

Launched in 2017, the mission aims to measure neutron star radii to within 5% accuracy, helping constrain the equation of state of matter at nuclear densities—conditions no terrestrial laboratory can recreate.
