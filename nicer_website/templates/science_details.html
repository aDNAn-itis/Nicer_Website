01 / THE BIG QUESTION

What happens
inside a neutron star?

Its size and mass reveal how matter responds under immense pressure.

A laboratory the Earth cannot build

Neutron stars compress stellar mass into a city-sized object. Their interiors provide a way to test models of dense matter beyond the conditions available in laboratories.

NICER measures changing X-ray brightness as hot regions rotate in and out of view. The shape of those pulses can constrain a star's mass and radius and, in turn, the properties of matter within it.
