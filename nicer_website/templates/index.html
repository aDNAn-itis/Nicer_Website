About the NICER Mission

The Neutron star Interior Composition Explorer (NICER) is an X-ray timing instrument aboard the International Space Station. NICER's primary science goal is to measure neutron star radii to within 5%, constraining the equation of state of matter at nuclear densities.

NICER consists of 56 paired SDD-detector plus concentrator optics, providing unprecedented sensitivity for X-ray timing measurements in the 0.2-12 keV energy range.

Science Goals

NICER enables groundbreaking science by measuring neutron star masses and radii with unprecedented precision. These measurements help constrain the equation of state of nuclear matter under extreme conditions.

Key scientific objectives include studying neutron star interior composition, testing general relativity in strong gravitational fields, and understanding the physics of accretion onto compact objects.
